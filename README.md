## 01. The Vision

Krynket Miles is an AI-powered leisure mobility platform designed to simplify vehicle discovery, flexible rentals, chauffeur services, and personalized road-trip experiences through a unified digital ecosystem.

The platform aims to bridge the gap between mobility accessibility and travel experiences by connecting users with suitable vehicles and professional drivers based on their travel requirements, preferences, destinations, and budgets.

Rather than treating mobility as a simple point-to-point transportation service, Krynket Miles focuses on making every journey more flexible, accessible, transparent, and experience-driven.

---

## 02. The Problem

Planning a road trip or accessing a vehicle for leisure travel often involves multiple disconnected services.

- Limited access to suitable vehicles for spontaneous journeys.
- Fragmented vehicle rental and chauffeur booking experiences.
- Unclear rental costs, additional charges, and pricing structures.
- Difficulty discovering suitable vehicles based on travel requirements.
- Lack of integrated tools for planning and managing leisure journeys.

## 03. The Solution

Krynket Miles brings vehicle discovery, rental flexibility, driver services, and intelligent travel planning into one unified platform.

Users can explore mobility options, compare suitable vehicles, estimate journey costs, and organize travel experiences through a streamlined interface.

## 05. How It Works

Krynket Miles transforms a conventional travel requirement into a streamlined mobility experience.

1. **Discover** — Enter your destination, travel requirements, and preferences.
2. **Explore** — Browse relevant vehicles and mobility options.
3. **Compare** — Evaluate pricing, vehicle details, and available services.
4. **Choose** — Select self-drive, chauffeur-driven, or driver-only services.
5. **Experience** — Organize your journey with a convenient mobility experience.
